# Spillguard terms of use

Last updated: 16 November 2026
Provider: Satyarth Shukla, contact satyarthshukla86@gmail.com

1. What the app is. Spillguard compares text on Jira Service Management requests and Confluence pages with restricted Confluence pages and acts on matches: it can move a public comment to an internal note, restrict an open Confluence page and show alerts to administrators.
2. It is a safety net, not a guarantee. Matching is based on copied text, codes, and rewordings found by a rule-based comparison. It will miss some rewordings and may block harmless text. You stay responsible for what is shown to your customers and for who can read your restricted pages.
3. Actions on your content. The app can delete a public comment after posting an internal copy, and can add a read restriction to a Confluence page (author, the site-admins group and the app keep access). It does not change request fields. Remove a page restriction in Confluence to undo it.
4. Installation and permissions. Only a site administrator installs the app. You approve the permissions shown at install. Re-indexing reads Confluence as the administrator who has the Spillguard page open, so it only knows restricted pages that administrator can see; the app does not remember which administrator that was.
5. Your data. See the privacy policy. We do not sell or share your data.
6. Availability. The app is provided as is, without warranty, and may change or stop at any time. It uses Atlassian preview features (the Teamwork Graph and the Rovo MCP module) that Atlassian can change.

8. Ending. You can uninstall the app at any time. We may stop providing it.
